Water Logged data credits
========================
MoonPhase.java adapts the lunar illumination algorithm from SunCalc 1.9.0:
https://github.com/mourner/suncalc/tree/v1.9.0
The BSD-2-Clause notice above is retained for this adaptation.

Weather and marine model data: Open-Meteo, https://open-meteo.com/
Licensed under CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Values are selected for the catch time, wind bearings converted to compass labels,
and model areas selected using rounded coordinates. They are model estimates.
API service terms: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms
This student pilot uses the non-commercial free service. A commercial release
requires a suitable paid service plan and compliance with provider terms.

Queensland tide predictions: Maritime Safety Queensland / Bureau of Meteorology,
provided through Queensland Open Data, https://www.data.qld.gov.au/
https://www.msq.qld.gov.au/Tides/Open-data
Licensed under CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Dataset links and licences are preserved in qld_tide_stations.json.
Gauge coordinates were converted from published degrees/minutes metadata.
Prediction rows are selected by catch time; level trend is derived from adjoining
10-minute heights. Heights are above Lowest Astronomical Tide (LAT). Predictions
are not actual water-level observations or a measure of tidal current.

Pilot 0.3 additional data and image credits
=========================================
Observed weather: Bureau of Meteorology, Copyright Commonwealth of Australia.
Documented public 72-hour observation feeds:
https://www.bom.gov.au/catalogue/data-feeds.shtml
https://www.bom.gov.au/copyright
These anonymous feeds are for personal/non-commercial pilot use, subject to
BOM's terms. No claim is made that they carry the CC BY licence used by the
Queensland Open Data datasets. Commercial use requires appropriate permission
or a licensed data service. The app requests public station identifiers only.

Observed total waves / Hs: Queensland Coastal Data System, Department of the
Environment, Tourism, Science and Innovation, Queensland Government.
https://www.data.qld.gov.au/dataset/coastal-data-system-near-real-time-wave-data
CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Public buoy positions are used to match a nearby site locally. Hs is retained as
total significant wave height, not relabelled as swell or maximum wave height.

Model data: Open-Meteo, with upstream providers including DWD, ECMWF,
Meteo-France, NOAA/NCEP and BOM as described in Open-Meteo's documentation.
The app labels model estimates separately from observations.

Species reference photographs and illustrations: individual creators listed in
species_catalogue.json and Species image credits in offline Help. Each file
retains its CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0 or public-domain terms. The app code does not
change the licences of those image files. Source pages and full licence links
are preserved per image. Generic icons are used where no suitable verified
freely licensed image has been bundled.
