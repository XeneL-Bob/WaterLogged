Welcome to Water Logged

Your fishing diary, stored on your device.

Log a catch

Tap New, search for a common or scientific name, then choose your fish. A star puts your favourite species first. The offline catalogue has 32 species and 30 licensed reference images, including photographs and illustrations. Tap a species image for its creator, licence and source. Pearl perch and spotted mackerel use generic icons. Confirm fish identification independently.

Enter a title, location name and quantity. Length, weight, photo, notes and conditions are optional. Tap the date and time buttons to record exactly when the fish was caught.

Valid changes auto-save after a short pause and when you leave. Save to Log saves immediately and opens your diary. Invalid edits do not replace the last valid saved entry.

GPS and photos

Get GPS asks for foreground location access and waits up to 25 seconds for a fresh location. Approximate access may reduce accuracy. If access is denied or no fix is available, you can still enter a location name.

Show map is enabled after a location is captured. It opens your map app with that mark. Take photo opens the device camera. Tap the saved photo to open a larger view, pinch to zoom, drag to move, and double tap to reset.

Your diary

Tap a record to reopen and edit it. Existing catches have a Delete button with confirmation. Choose a day to see that day's entries, or All dates to see the full log. Sort offers catch date newest first and length lowest first; unmeasured fish follow measured fish. Your chosen sort order is remembered.

Privacy and sharing

Share shows a preview containing the title, location name, catch time, length, quantity and catch status. Exact coordinates, photos and notes are excluded. Avoid entering secret fishing marks in the location name if you intend to share it.

Records and photos stay in private app storage. This pilot has no cloud account. Automatic Android backup is disabled. Create an encrypted backup in Settings to keep an external copy; uninstalling or clearing storage removes the on-device diary.

Automatic conditions

New catches request a fresh foreground GPS fix. Choose location / test point lets you enter coordinates or select the public Mooloolaba example. Editing the place name changes only its label. On Home, Refresh my location captures the device location again.

Weather prefers a nearby supported BOM station observation at or before the catch, at most 60 minutes old. The pilot includes 16 Queensland stations. Missing values use labelled Open-Meteo model estimates. Sustained wind and gusts are separate fields.

Total significant wave height (Hs) prefers a nearby supported Queensland buoy observation at or before the catch, at most 90 minutes old. The pilot includes 27 buoy locations. A missing observation uses model total waves. Swell is a separate modelled component. Hs, swell and tide are different measurements; a swell graph does not establish the tide height.

Queensland tide predictions come from MSQ/BOM through Queensland Open Data. Heights are above Lowest Astronomical Tide (LAT); published times are Queensland UTC+10. The nearest supported gauge within 50 km is selected automatically. Use Tide gauge to choose another nearby gauge on your waterway. Rising/Falling/Turning describes water level, not tidal current. Freshwater skips waves, swell and tides.

Each automatic field shows its source, applicable time and retrieval time. Named station/buoy/gauge labels and model labels show their source distance. Moon phase is calculated offline using SunCalc. Weather/marine model and location-name requests use GPS rounded to two decimals, roughly a kilometre. Exact marks are matched locally to public station IDs and stay in private app storage unless you deliberately open a map.

Correct any field manually, including clearing it. Refresh conditions requests fresh responses without overwriting those corrections. Cached fallback readings keep their retrieval times and are labelled. Changing time or position invalidates automatic values from the old context; manual corrections survive. Reopening an old catch keeps its saved snapshot. Unsupported dates, missing data and outages leave manual entry available.

Valid edits auto-save after a short pause and on leaving/backgrounding; Save to Log saves immediately. Catches and metadata use private Room storage, camera images use private files, and provider responses use a separate disposable cache. Automatic Android backup and device transfer are disabled. An encrypted backup exported in Settings can restore the diary after reinstalling. GitHub backs up project code, not catch records.

Fishing rules

This app does not calculate legal possession limits or detect marine park boundaries. Always check current rules, species identification, seasonal closures and protected areas using the relevant official authority.

Backup and spreadsheet export

Settings â†’ Backup, restore & CSV export lets you create a password-protected .wlog file containing catches, photos, sessions, favourites, custom species and snapshots. Choose its destination yourself; a cloud document provider receives the encrypted file if selected. Keep the password separately; it is never saved and cannot be recovered.

Restore adds missing records without replacing existing catches. Favourite choices are restored from the file. Trips active in the backup return as ended at backup time. CSV is unencrypted and excludes coordinates by default, but includes location names and notes. CSV is for analysis, not full recovery. Pilot limits: 256 MB payload, 32 MB per photo and 20,000 records per type.

Sessions, search and automatic values

Fishing sessions on Home/Settings let you start/end a trip, reuse location/bait/method defaults and view duration and kept/released totals. Each catch gets its own timestamp and conditions. Trips with no fish remain in history. Totals are diary counts, not possession-limit checks.

Search & filters combines species/aliases/location/notes, date range, status and method. The Through date includes the whole day. Both sort choices still run in the database. Clear all removes active filters.

Use automatic value again releases one manually corrected or cleared field; other corrections remain. Source labels show Observed, Forecast, Prediction, Calculated, Manual or Cached and the applicable age/time. Tide charts show actual gauge-day predictions above LAT with AEST times and a catch marker; their saved curve works offline.

Custom species

Add a custom species saves a reusable common name, optional scientific name and regional aliases locally, marked unverified. It can be searched and favourited and is included in encrypted backups. No review request is sent externally. Generic icons remain until a verified licensed reference is available.
